# SuriBagoClick

**Slogan:** Think Before You Click. Protect Before You Trust.

A student-built, browser-based digital awareness website that helps users practice recognizing common online scam warning signs.

## Run the website
1. Download and extract `SuriBagoClick.zip`.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
3. Internet is only needed for Google Fonts and the external official-source links. The interactive tools work locally.

## Features
- Responsive professional startup-style website.
- Scam phrase checker with explanations and safer next steps.
- Three fictional scam-awareness scenarios with feedback.
- Five-question knowledge quiz with score and explanations.
- Safety routine and guidance on what to do if a user suspects a scam.
- Links to official Philippine institutions.

## Important limitations and privacy
- The checker uses a small, transparent list of phrase matches. It does not verify URLs, identify senders, connect to a threat-intelligence database, or determine whether a message is genuinely fraudulent.
- Message text is processed in the browser and is not transmitted or saved by this prototype.
- Do not enter passwords, OTPs, PINs, account numbers, or other sensitive personal information.
- The scenarios and sample links are fictional and use the reserved `.example` domain.
- This is an educational prototype, not an official government service, bank tool, or substitute for professional assistance.

## Suggested project explanation
SuriBagoClick addresses the risk that people may respond to suspicious online messages without recognizing warning signs. It turns awareness into practice through a phrase-based checker, fictional scenarios, and a knowledge quiz. The goal is not to label every message as safe or fraudulent, but to encourage users to pause, check, verify through trusted channels, and protect private information.

## Technology
HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. No backend, account registration, or paid service is required.
