A SIMPLE SAFETY ROUTINE

Make these four steps
a daily habit.

When a message feels suspicious, do not let pressure decide for you. Take a moment to verify.

01STOP

Pause before acting.

Do not rush to click, reply, download, or pay.

02CHECK

Look for red flags.

Notice unusual requests, pressure, and suspicious links.

03VERIFY

Use a trusted channel.

Contact the organization using its official app or website you find independently.

04PROTECT

Keep secrets private.

Never share passwords, PINs, or OTPs. Report suspicious activity.

IF YOU THINK YOU WERE SCAMMED

Act quickly.
Get trusted help.

Contact your bank or e-wallet provider immediately if money or account access may be at risk. Use official contact details from their verified app or website.

01Secure your accountsChange compromised passwords using the official service.
02Contact your providerAsk your bank or e-wallet to secure the account or transaction.
03Keep evidenceSave screenshots, transaction references, and messages. Do not edit evidence.
04Report through official channelsSeek guidance from the appropriate Philippine authorities.
